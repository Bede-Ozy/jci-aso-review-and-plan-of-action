💡 Executive Summary: Key Findings & Recommendations
Feedback from the 2027 Plan of Action survey reveals an active, enthusiastic membership where 100% of respondents consider themselves involved (50% very active, 50% somewhat active). Members are asking for practical upgrades in 2027: launching a hands-on Business Clinic (68.2%) to support member businesses, building international partnerships with sister JCI chapters (63.6%), modernizing the Quality Leadership Value (QLV) workshops, creating regular mentorship opportunities with Past Presidents and Senators, and retiring World Down Syndrome Day so volunteer energy and funds can go toward projects members love most.

Projects to Reconsider & What Members Value Most

Data-backed recommendations on retiring low-performing projects and what benefits members appreciate most

⚠️ Project Recommended to Retire: World Down Syndrome Day

3 Direct Votes

In Question 18, World Down Syndrome Day (WDSD) was the only project members explicitly suggested stopping. This aligns directly with the survey numbers: it had the lowest turnout in the chapter (only 22.7% attended) and the lowest average rating (3.23★).

“Unless we are channeling our efforts towards schools or campuses where people with Down syndrome are abundant and directly interacting with them by providing every support when necessary, it's one project I feel we should use the resources for another activity.”
— Response R003 (Active Member, 1–3 Years in JCI Aso)
Our Recommendation:
Do not run World Down Syndrome Day as a standalone project in 2027. Redirect volunteer hours, energy, and funds into the projects members are eagerly asking for — such as the new Business Clinic and community health outreaches.

What Members Are Saying: Main Feedback & Ideas

We reviewed all written suggestions from members in the survey and grouped them into three clear, practical areas:

📋 Better Screening for New Members
“More of onboarding, I think this should be stricter to ascertain quality of members and commitment.”
Action: Introduce a simple 30-day trial period so new members understand the commitment before induction.
📍 Improving Member Attendance at Events
“Membership participation in indigenous L.O projects. Members do not attend in their numbers; if worked on, Aso will reach more intending members.”
Action: Set clear attendance targets for each committee and celebrate members who show up regularly.
🤝 Connecting Newer Members with Past Leaders & Senators
“There should be an avenue creation where younger members can meet the older members, I mean past presidents as well as the senators.”
Action: Host regular informal dinners and mentoring chats pairing newer members with Senators and Past Presidents.
Section 5.0

5 Key Priorities & Quarterly Roadmap for 2027

Practical, step-by-step actions for the incoming 2027 Executive Board
01

Priority 1: Launch the JCI Aso Business Clinic

Responding directly to the #1 requested program (68.2%), establish a regular Business Clinic where member-entrepreneurs get practical advice on business registration, financial planning, pitch presentations, and one-on-one mentorship from experienced business leaders.

Lead: VP Business & Entrepreneurship Target: 4 Clinics, 40+ Member Businesses Supported
02

Priority 2: Secure Corporate Sponsorships & Grants

Set up a dedicated team to pursue corporate partnerships and CSR funding (backed by 63.6% of members). This will eliminate out-of-pocket member levies for major community projects like Save a Soul and the Secondary School Debate.

Lead: Director of Strategic Partnerships Target: 3 Corporate Partners, 2 Grants Won
03

Priority 3: Retire World Down Syndrome Day & Upgrade QLV Training

Officially retire World Down Syndrome Day to free up energy and funds for the new Business Clinic. Upgrade Quality Leadership Value (QLV) from lecture-style sessions into lively, practical workshops, raising member satisfaction from 3.77★ to over 4.50★.

Lead: VP Individual Development Target: QLV Rating > 4.50★
04

Priority 4: Start a Mentorship Program with Senators & Past Presidents

Connect the 54.5% of members in their first 1–3 years with experienced Alumni, Senators, and Past Presidents. Hold quarterly informal dinners focused on career growth, leadership advice, and JCI traditions.

Lead: Immediate Past President & Alumni Liaison Target: 4 Mentoring Sessions, All New Members Paired
05

Priority 5: Improve New Member Onboarding & Event Promotion

Require a 30-day orientation period before induction to make sure new members are genuinely committed and ready to attend events. At the same time, boost social media storytelling so the public sees the great work JCI Aso is doing.

Lead: VP Membership & Director of PR Target: 80%+ Active Attendance by Inducted Members
