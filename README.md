# JCI Aso – Plan of Action 2027: Member Insights, Analytics & Strategic Recommendations

A comprehensive data analytics, visualization, and strategic planning suite built for **Junior Chamber International (JCI) Aso (Abuja, Nigeria)** to inform the **Plan of Action 2027**.

---

## 🌟 Deliverables & Repository Structure

| Deliverable | File | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Interactive Web Report** | [`index.html`](./index.html) | Full-screen, responsive web dashboard with sticky navigation, interactive project filter, 2×2 decision matrix, and quarterly roadmap. |
| **Official Executive PDF** | [`Plan_of_Action_Recommendation_Report_2027.pdf`](./Plan_of_Action_Recommendation_Report_2027.pdf) | Publication-grade 6-page strategic brief with JCI branding, KPI cards, portfolio performance matrix, qualitative feedback, and 5 Action Pillars. |
| **Educational Analysis Notebook** | [`survey_insights_and_recommendations_analysis.ipynb`](./survey_insights_and_recommendations_analysis.ipynb) | Step-by-step Jupyter Notebook teaching survey analytics, dual-grain modeling, cross-tabs, 2×2 quadrant frameworks, and thematic clustering. |
| **Data Cleaning Notebook** | [`data_cleaning_and_grain_modeling.ipynb`](./data_cleaning_and_grain_modeling.ipynb) | Educational notebook demonstrating multi-select unnesting, string normalization, and grain modeling. |
| **Raw Survey Export (Excel)** | [`Plan_of_Action.xlsx`](./Plan_of_Action.xlsx) | Original survey responses dataset. |

## 📊 Key Insights Summary

1. **Member Base & Demographics:**
   - **86.4% Active members** (18–40 years), **13.6% Alumni** (40+ years).
   - **54.5%** are in their first 1–3 years of membership.
   - **100% active engagement** (50% Very Active, 50% Somewhat Active).

2. **Project Performance: 2×2 Attendance vs. Satisfaction Matrix:**
   - **★ Top Performers (High Turnout > 60%, High Rating ≥ 4.20★):**
     - *Baba and Yara* (81.8% Turnout, 4.64 ★)
     - *Save a Soul* (68.2% Turnout, 4.27 ★)
     - *International Women's Day* (68.2% Turnout, 4.23 ★)
   - **◆ High Quality, Lower Turnout (< 60% Turnout, High Rating ≥ 4.20★):**
     - *Secondary School Debate* (54.5% Turnout, 4.55 ★)
     - *Membership Development Summit (MDS)* (59.1% Turnout, 4.14 ★)
   - **▲ Popular, Needs Improvement (> 60% Turnout, Lower Rating < 4.20★):**
     - *Quality Leadership Value (QLV)* (63.6% Turnout, 3.77 ★)
   - **▼ Consider Retiring (< 60% Turnout, Lower Rating < 4.20★):**
     - *Educate a Child* (50.0% Turnout, 3.86 ★)
     - *World Down Syndrome Day* (22.7% Turnout, 3.23 ★)

3. **Top Member Desires for 2027:**
   - **JCI Aso Business Clinic (68.2%):** Entrepreneurship and business support.
   - **International Sister Chapter Twinning (63.6%):** Cross-border chapter exchanges.
   - **Alumni-Youth Mentoring Dinners (54.5%):** Bridge between younger members, past presidents, and senators.
